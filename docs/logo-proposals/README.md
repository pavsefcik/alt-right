# Alt Right — logo proposals

Three rounds of icon ideas for the Finder utility.

**Chosen: 04 Space.** The final version lives in [`final/`](final/) — the operator's own
`icon-1024.png` geometry, flat blue replaced by a gradient, eight colourways to pick from.
See `final/sheet.png` and `final/variants-sheet.png` (rendered locally, see below).
The rest of this file is the history of how we got there.

> Rendered output — every `*.png` and `*.icns` here — is git-ignored; this folder only
> commits the sources (SVG, HTML, scripts). Run `render.sh` or the commands near the
> bottom to produce the sheets and icon sizes again.

## Round 2 — fun (chosen: 04)

| # | Name | Idea | Why it might win |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **Bounce** | The elbow arrow from the first round, given eyes, a 7° lean and a twinkle | Keeps the existing blue + arrow DNA, just stops being stiff |
| 02 | **Peek** | A folder with a face; the back panel still reads "Finder folder" | Warmest, most obviously "Finder"; very legible at 32 px |
| 03 | **Click** | Pointer hitting a click-burst | Loudest, most energy; the only one that is purely "right-click" |
| 04 | **Space** | White triangles with dot eyes on a starfield (your reference) | **Chosen** — highest contrast, retro, memorable, and the only one that is a wordmark-ish mark |
| 05 | **Keycap** | The Alt key mid-press, engraved with a right arrow | Pun does the work: Alt + Right in one object; tactile |

Each proposal is a full 1024 squircle with a transparent surround, plus 512/256/128/64/32
renders, so you can see how it holds up in the Dock and in Finder's toolbar.

Notes and known weak spots:

- **01** — the head/shaft joint leaves a small notch under the arrowhead. Reads as a bird
  beak to some people; that is either the charm or the problem.
- **02** — safest crowd-pleaser, but "cute folder" is a well-worn idea; the blush is doing a
  lot of the work.
- **03** — at 32 px the burst and the pointer start to merge into one yellow-white blob.
- **04** — closest to your screenshot, so it borrows your taste rather than adding to it;
  also the least "menu bar app" of the five.
- **05** — the concept is the strongest, the execution the most fiddly: the engraved arrow is
  soft at small sizes and the keycap needs to stay crisp.

## Menu bar

The app also needs a 1-colour template glyph. `menubar/` has three (18 pt) that match the
proposals: `elbow.svg`, `chevron.svg`, `click.svg`. They are drawn black on transparent and
re-render to `*.png` at 18 px. macOS only uses the alpha channel for template images.

## Turning one into the real app icon

The app currently ships `AltRight/AppIcon.icns`, generated by `scripts/gen_icon.swift` (an
SF Symbol arrow, no tile). Two ways to adopt a proposal:

1. **Tile version** (what these SVGs are): convert the chosen `icon-1024.png` into an
   `.icns` and drop it in place of `AltRight/AppIcon.icns`:

   ```bash
   mkdir -p /tmp/AltRight.iconset
   for s in 16 32 64 128 256 512; do
     sips -z $s $s fun/01-bounce/icon-1024.png --out /tmp/AltRight.iconset/icon_${s}x${s}.png
     d=$((s*2))
     sips -z $d $d fun/01-bounce/icon-1024.png --out /tmp/AltRight.iconset/icon_${s}x${s}@2x.png
   done
   iconutil -c icns /tmp/AltRight.iconset -o AltRight/AppIcon.icns
   ```

2. **Tile-free version** (matches the current icon and your reference): the mark without the
   squircle, drawn in `scripts/gen_icon.swift` the way the arrow is drawn today.

Say which one you like and I will do the conversion, the dock/menu-bar check, and the
small-size cleanup.
